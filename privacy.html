Privacy and saves

These independent games are maintained by timothy-le7. They are free to play, without accounts, advertising, analytics, chat, or in-game purchases.

Your progress stays on your device

The game code keeps saves and settings in this browser or installed web app. It does not upload your moves, saved games, or imported backup files. Exporting creates a backup file you choose where to keep or share. A different browser or installed app may have a separate save.

Website hosting

On the published website, GitHub Pages serves the game files and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is separate from the games’ local saves. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. This notice does not promise that the hosting provider collects no data.

Removing your saved data

Use your browser or installed app settings to remove this site’s stored data. This can erase saves for several games in that browser or app. Export any backups you want to keep first. Deleting local saves does not delete the hosting provider’s security logs.

About these games

The names and artwork here identify this independent project. The games are not presented as official products, endorsed adaptations, or licensed versions of another studio’s games.
